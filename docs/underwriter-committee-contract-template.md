## Recitals

**WHEREAS:**

A. The Committee serves as the interim legal principal and fiduciary wrapper for the Venture Vision Ubuntu (VVU) autonomous execution system;

B. The VVU system requires authorized underwriters to provide financial backing for village-scale ROSCA operations within FSCA regulatory parameters;

C. The Committee holds full accountability for system outcomes and maintains compliance with the Financial Advisory and Intermediary Services Act;

D. This agreement establishes the terms under which the Underwriter shall provide underwriting services to the VVU system during the interim period;

E. Upon registration of the UBUNTUctrl Trust, this agreement shall automatically novate to the Trust as the successor principal;

**NOW THEREFORE**, the parties agree as follows:

*This agreement is made between VAGUELY VANITY LLC (Pty) Ltd trading as Venture Vision Ubuntu (the "Company") and the Underwriter, with the Company operating under the governance of the UBUNTUctrl Ad-Hoc Committee.*

## 1. Definitions

**"Committee"** means the Ad-Hoc UBUNTUctrl Committee constituted as the interim legal principal;

**"FSCA"** means the Financial Sector Conduct Authority of South Africa;

**"GovernanceAction"** means a cryptographically signed approval issued by the Committee pursuant to the VVU governance protocol;

**"Policy Framework"** means the risk management and operational parameters approved by the Committee and enforced by the VVU execution engine;

**"Pool"** means a village-scale ROSCA pool executed through the VVU system;

**"SafeStakes"** means the VVU system's execution engine that enforces deterministic rules within approved parameters;

**"System"** means the Venture Vision Ubuntu autonomous execution infrastructure;

**"Trust"** means the UBUNTUctrl Trust to be registered with the Master of the High Court;

**"Underwriter"** means the licensed financial services provider identified above;

**"VVU"** means the Venture Vision Ubuntu system for autonomous financial execution.

## 2. Appointment and Authority

### 2.1 Exclusive Appointment

The Committee hereby appoints the Underwriter as an authorized underwriter for the VVU system, subject to the terms and conditions herein.

### 2.2 Scope of Authority

The Underwriter is authorized to:

2.2.1 Provide underwriting services for Pool operations within the approved Policy Framework;  
2.2.2 Issue financial guarantees backed by the Underwriter's capital adequacy requirements;  
2.2.3 Verify pool health metrics through the VVU telemetry system;  
2.2.4 Respond to default events as triggered by SafeStakes execution;  
2.2.5 Report to the Committee on underwriting performance and risk metrics.

### 2.3 Limitations on Authority

The Underwriter shall not:

2.3.1 Modify the Policy Framework without GovernanceAction approval;  
2.3.2 Override SafeStakes deterministic execution;  
2.3.3 Engage in activities outside the approved underwriting scope;  
2.3.4 Make representations regarding system autonomy or governance.

---

## 3. Underwriting Obligations

### 3.1 Capital Adequacy

The Underwriter shall maintain capital adequacy in accordance with FSCA requirements for the aggregate exposure under this agreement.

### 3.2 Risk Assessment

The Underwriter shall:

3.2.1 Evaluate pool risk metrics provided by VVU telemetry;  
3.2.2 Maintain independent risk modeling capabilities;  
3.2.3 Report material risk changes to the Committee within 24 hours.

### 3.3 Claims Processing

Upon default event notification from SafeStakes:

3.3.1 Verify claim validity against Policy Framework parameters;  
3.3.2 Process eligible claims within 5 business days;  
3.3.3 Maintain complete audit trails of all claim decisions.

### 3.4 Reporting Requirements

The Underwriter shall provide:

3.4.1 Monthly performance reports to the Committee;  
3.4.2 Quarterly FSCA regulatory reporting;  
3.4.3 Immediate notification of any regulatory compliance issues.

---

## 4. Committee Obligations

### 4.1 Governance Oversight

The Committee shall:

4.1.1 Maintain the Policy Framework within FSCA parameters;  
4.1.2 Issue GovernanceActions for policy modifications;  
4.1.3 Monitor system execution through SafeStakes telemetry;  
4.1.4 Ensure capital adequacy across all authorized underwriters.

### 4.2 System Integrity

The Committee guarantees that:

4.2.1 SafeStakes execution remains deterministic within approved parameters;  
4.2.2 All policy changes require GovernanceAction approval;  
4.2.3 The system maintains FSCA compliance at all times.

### 4.3 Support and Cooperation

The Committee shall provide:

4.3.1 Access to VVU telemetry and risk metrics;  
4.3.2 GovernanceAction approvals for underwriting modifications;  
4.3.3 Dispute resolution mechanisms for underwriting claims.

## 7. Liability and Indemnification

### 7.1 Underwriter Liability

The Underwriter shall be liable for:

7.1.1 Failure to meet valid claims within Policy Framework parameters;  
7.1.2 Breach of FSCA regulatory requirements;  
7.1.3 Negligent underwriting practices.

### 7.2 Committee Liability

The Committee shall be liable for:

7.2.1 System execution outside approved Policy Framework;  
7.2.2 Failure to maintain governance integrity;  
7.2.3 Breach of fiduciary duties as interim principal.

### 7.3 Mutual Indemnification

Each party shall indemnify the other against losses arising from their respective breaches of this agreement.

## 8. Termination

### 8.1 Termination Rights

Either party may terminate this agreement:

8.1.1 Upon 90 days written notice;  
8.1.2 Immediately upon material breach;  
8.1.3 Upon loss of regulatory authorization;  
8.1.4 Upon insolvency of either party.

### 8.2 Transition Obligations

Upon termination:

8.2.1 The Underwriter shall continue to service existing claims for 24 months;  
8.2.2 The Committee shall facilitate transition to alternative underwriters;  
8.2.3 All confidential information shall remain protected.
