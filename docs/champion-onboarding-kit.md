### 2. Quick Start Guide

#### Step 1: Your First Game (10 minutes)
```
Choose: Pool Simulator (recommended first)

Why? Shows community resilience patterns you already know intuitively.

How to play:
1. Start game
2. Make decisions as you would in real life
3. See what patterns emerge
4. Share insights with your group
```

#### Step 2: Review Insights (5 minutes)
```
Look for:
• What surprised you about your decision-making?
• What patterns match your real experience?
• What would help your community?

Don't worry about "right" answers. This is about recognition.
```

#### Step 3: Share with Community (15 minutes)
```
Use this script:

"Sawubona everyone. I tried this community simulation tool.

It showed me [one insight that resonated].

What do you think? Should we try it as a group?"

This starts the conversation.
```

### 3. Champion Resources

#### Communication Templates
```
Introducing to Your Group:

"Sawubona. I've been testing a tool that helps communities understand their strengths better. It's like a mirror for how we work together. No money involved. Just learning.

Would you be interested in trying it?"

Sharing Insights:

"The tool showed our group has strong [positive pattern]. That's why we've been successful.

It also highlighted [area for growth]. Maybe we can work on that together."
```

#### Troubleshooting Guide
```
Common Questions:

Q: "Is this a bank?"
A: "No. It's a tool that helps communities see their trust patterns."

Q: "Do we have to pay?"
A: "No. Free to learn. We only ask for feedback."

Q: "Is my data safe?"
A: "Yes. South African data laws. You control everything."

Q: "What if people don't like their insights?"
A: "Insights are private by default. Sharing is optional."
```

## 🚩 Champion Safety Protocols

### Trust Protection

- Monitor for misuse
- Provide clear boundaries
- Regular check-ins for well-being
- Exit pathways for champions

- Redirect harmful dynamics
- Escalate concerns appropriately
- Celebrate collective wins
