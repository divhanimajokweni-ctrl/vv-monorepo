# BayWater Corporation Q1 2026 Board Report

## Executive Summary

In Q1 2026, BayWater Corporation successfully deployed its complete smart water infrastructure system, marking a significant milestone in our journey toward intelligent water management. All core components are now operational, providing secure and immutable monitoring of water systems.

## Operational Status

Our smart water infrastructure is built on three foundational services:

1. **SafeKrypte**: Provides cryptographic signing for all system data, ensuring authenticity and preventing tampering.

2. **SafeStakes**: Handles secure transaction processing and escrow custody for water-related financial operations.

3. **Mainframe (Triad Collector)**: Continuously monitors three critical water metrics:
   - Flow rate (checked every minute)
   - Pressure levels (checked every 5 minutes) 
   - Leak detection (checked every minute)

All services are deployed on the Railway platform and are running 24/7.
