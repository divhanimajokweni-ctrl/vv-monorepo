# 🌍 Ubuntu Pools — WhatsApp Community Ignition Scripts

## Phase 15 Launch Campaign (90-Day Sprint)

**Strategy:** Games → Stories → Trust → Adoption

---

## 🎯 Campaign Overview

### Primary Objective
Activate **learning loops** through community storytelling, not direct sales.

### Success Metrics
- 500+ game sessions in first 30 days
- 20+ village champions recruited
- 15+ community stories generated

### Channel Strategy
- **Group Messages**: Daily engagement
- **Private DMs**: Champion recruitment
- **Story Sharing**: User-generated content

## 👥 Champion Onboarding Script

### Private DM Sequence

#### Initial Response
```
Thank you for being a Village Champion! 🌟

This means:
• You get early access to insights
• Your feedback shapes the platform
• Your village benefits first

Ready to start? I'll guide you through.

First: What's your role in your community?
```

#### Step 1: Understanding
```
Got it. As [their role], you have unique insight into how your community works.

Our tool helps surface patterns that are hard to see.

Would you like me to send you a private link to try the first game?
```

#### Step 2: First Game
```
Here's your champion access link:
[PRIVATE LINK]

Play the Pool Simulator first - it shows community resilience patterns.

After playing, reply with:
1. What surprised you?
2. What would help your community?
```

#### Step 3: Debrief
```
Thank you for that feedback!

Based on what you shared, [personalized insight about their community].

This is exactly the kind of pattern we're helping communities understand.

Ready to introduce this to your group?
```

## 🎯 Conversion Funnels

### Game → Story → Champion → Village

1. **Game Play** (0 friction)
2. **Insight Share** (social validation)
3. **Champion Recruitment** (leadership activation)
4. **Village Onboarding** (community adoption)

### WhatsApp-Specific Triggers

- **High engagement** → Champion DM
- **Story shared** → Community spotlight
- **Question asked** → Personal response
- **Challenge completed** → Tournament invite

## 🚩 Safety & Compliance

### POPIA Compliance
- No personal data collection without consent
- All insights aggregated/anonymized
- Clear opt-out at any time

### Community Safety
- No individual shaming
- Collective success focus
- Cultural sensitivity maintained

### Growth Safety
- No aggressive conversion
- Trust-building first
- Value demonstration required
