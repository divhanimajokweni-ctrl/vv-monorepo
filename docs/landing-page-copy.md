## 🚩 Risk Mitigation

### Trust Risks
- Never mention credit/finance in headlines
- Always emphasize learning, not judgment
- Make sharing optional and controlled
- Highlight data sovereignty prominently

### Engagement Risks
- Keep games under 15 minutes
- Provide clear progress indicators
- Allow saving and resuming
- Make insights immediately valuable

### Scale Risks
- Start with capacity monitoring
- Have fallback for high traffic
- Monitor for gaming the system
- Regular community feedback loops
