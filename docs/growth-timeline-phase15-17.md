## 🚩 Risk Mitigation Timeline

### Months 1-3: Foundation Risks
- **Platform instability** → Daily monitoring, rapid iteration
- **Cultural misalignment** → Community feedback loops, adaptation
- **Champion dropout** → Support system, recognition program

### Months 4-8: Scale Risks
- **Engagement dilution** → Segmentation, personalization
- **Quality compromise** → Standards maintenance, quality gates
- **Community conflicts** → Moderation systems, conflict resolution

### Months 9-12: Complexity Risks
- **Technical debt** → Architecture review, refactoring cycles
- **Community fragmentation** → Integration features, shared spaces
- **Sustainability concerns** → Business model development, partnership growth
