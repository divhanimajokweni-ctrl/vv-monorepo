# ✅ Phase 16 Tournament Communications Pack

**Village-Facing Messaging**

---

## 🎯 Core Framing

> **"This is not about winning.  
> It's about learning how we manage together."**

---

## 🏡 Village Announcement (WhatsApp / Meeting)

```
Village update 🌍

Our village will take part in a learning challenge with other villages.

We'll use a pool‑management game to see:
• how we handle stress
• how we protect the group
• how we make decisions together

No money involved.
No individual scores.
Only village‑level learning.

This helps us grow stronger before real problems happen.
```

---

## 🏆 Tournament Rules (Simple & Clear)

*   Participation is voluntary
*   No financial rewards or penalties
*   Results are aggregated at village level
*   Focus is on learning, not ranking individuals
*   Insights are shared openly with the village

---

## 📊 Results Communication (Post‑Tournament)

```
Thank you to everyone who participated 🙏

What we learned as a village:
• We manage emergencies well
• We rely on buffers early

These insights help us prepare for real‑life challenges.
Nothing affects anyone's money or status.
```

## ⚠️ Risk Communication

### Addressing Concerns
```
Some questions we've heard:

Q: "Will this affect my money?"
A: "No. This is learning only."

Q: "Do I have to participate?"
A: "No. It's completely voluntary."

Q: "What if our village doesn't do well?"
A: "This isn't about winning. It's about understanding our patterns to improve them."
```

## 📈 Success Metrics

### Participation Goals
- **Individual:** 60-80% village participation
- **Completion:** 70% of participants finish games
- **Engagement:** Positive feedback >80%

### Learning Outcomes
- **Insights shared:** Village-level learnings documented
- **Action items:** 2-3 improvement areas identified

## 🎭 Cultural Adaptation Notes

### Language Considerations
- Use local languages where possible
- Respect cultural metaphors for pools/governance
- Adapt examples to local context

### Community Sensitivity
- Frame as "learning together" not "competition"
- Emphasize collective benefits over individual achievements
- Allow for cultural opt-out preferences

### Champion Support
- Provide champions with local cultural guidance
- Enable flexible communication styles
- Support multiple participation formats
