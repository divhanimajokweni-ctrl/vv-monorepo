# ✅ Ubuntu Pools — Champion Onboarding Guide

---

## 🌱 What Is a Champion?

A Champion is a **trusted community member** who helps others explore Ubuntu Pools safely and confidently.

You are **not selling** anything.  
You are **opening a door to learning**.

---

## 🎯 Your Role

*   Introduce the pool‑management game
*   Reassure members (no money, no risk)
*   Encourage participation
*   Help explain insights if asked
*   Model respectful use

---

## 🛡️ What You Must Emphasise

✅ No real money involved  
✅ No penalties or punishments  
✅ No individual rankings  
✅ Everyone controls their data  
✅ Learning comes first

---

## 🗣️ How to Explain It Simply

> "It's like practising fire drills before there's a fire."

or

> "It helps us see how we react under pressure — safely."

---

## ❌ What Not to Do

*   Don't promise credit or rewards
*   Don't pressure anyone to join
*   Don't interpret insights as judgment
*   Don't compare individuals publicly

---

## 🧭 When Questions Come Up

If you don't know the answer:

> "Let's check together."

You are not expected to be an expert.

---

## 🌍 Why This Matters

Strong communities fail less.  
Prepared groups survive shocks.  
Ubuntu Pools helps us **learn before crisis**.

---

## 📋 Your Champion Journey

### Week 1: Introduction
- Meet with your village/community group
- Share the learning opportunity
- Answer initial questions
- Invite voluntary participation

### Week 2: Support
- Help members get started
- Be available for questions
- Share your own experience
- Encourage completion

### Week 3: Reflection
- Discuss village-level insights
- Identify learning opportunities
- Plan community improvements
- Celebrate participation

### Ongoing: Leadership
- Continue supporting new members
- Share success stories
- Help with community governance
- Represent village in tournaments
