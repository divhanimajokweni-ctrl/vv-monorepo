## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this MoU is to establish a **non‑exclusive pilot collaboration** to evaluate the use of **community‑level, game‑based behavioural insights** to support:

*   Financial literacy
*   Collective governance capacity
*   Early risk visibility in informal savings groups

This pilot is **educational and observational only** and does not involve real financial stakes.

---

## 2. Scope of the Pilot

The pilot will include:

*   Participation of selected communities/villages
*   Use of Ubuntu Pools' **financial literacy games**
*   Participation in **Phase 16 village‑level tournaments**
*   Generation of **aggregated, anonymised behavioural insights**

The pilot **excludes**:

*   Real money transactions
*   Individual credit decisions
*   Automated enforcement or penalties

---

## 3. Roles & Responsibilities

### Ubuntu Pools will:

*   Provide access to the platform and games
*   Ensure POPIA‑compliant data handling
*   Supply aggregated insight reports
*   Maintain explainable AI outputs
*   Support onboarding and champion training

### Partner will:

*   Facilitate community access
*   Support informed consent processes
*   Use insights ethically and responsibly
*   Refrain from identifying or profiling individuals

---

## 4. Data Governance & Ethics

*   All participation is **voluntary**
*   All data collection is **consent‑based**
*   Only **derived behavioural signals** are shared
*   No raw telemetry or personal data is transferred
*   Members retain the right to **erasure of game‑derived data**
*   Insights are **advisory, not determinative**

Ubuntu Pools retains platform IP.  
Partner retains no ownership of individual‑level behavioural data.

---

## 5. Compliance & Regulation

This pilot:

*   Does not constitute gambling
*   Does not involve financial consideration
*   Does not replace regulated financial decision‑making
*   Is compliant with POPIA principles

---

## 6. Reporting & Evaluation

Ubuntu Pools will provide:

*   Periodic aggregated insight summaries
*   Educational and governance trend analysis
*   Pilot evaluation report at conclusion

---

## 7. Termination

Either party may terminate this MoU with **30 days' written notice**.

---

## 8. Non‑Exclusivity

This MoU is non‑exclusive and does not restrict either party from engaging in similar initiatives.
