# ✅ Ubuntu Pools: Regulatory Overview (One‑Page)

---

## What Ubuntu Pools Is

Ubuntu Pools is a **community trust and governance platform** that supports informal savings groups through education, simulations, and collective insight.

---

## Use of Games

*   Games are **financial literacy simulations**
*   No real money, prizes, or stakes are involved
*   Outcomes do **not** affect real financial positions
*   Games are used to surface **behavioural learning signals**

---

## Gambling Classification

Ubuntu Pools games:

*   Do not involve wagering
*   Do not offer prizes of economic value
*   Do not allow staking of money or assets
*   Are educational simulations only

Therefore, they **do not meet the definition of gambling** under South African law.

---

## Data Protection & POPIA

*   Explicit, informed consent
*   Minimal data collection
*   Derived signals only
*   Right to erasure
*   No sale of personal behavioural data
*   Explainable AI outputs

---

## Financial Regulation

*   Ubuntu Pools does not issue credit automatically
*   No financial decisions are made solely on game data
*   Insights are advisory and human‑interpreted
*   Platform supports, not replaces, regulated entities

---

## Summary

Ubuntu Pools is a **preventative, educational, and ethical system** designed to strengthen community governance and financial resilience without introducing regulatory risk.

---

## Key Regulatory Distinctions

| Aspect | Ubuntu Pools Approach | Regulatory Risk |
|--------|----------------------|-----------------|
| **Gambling** | No stakes, no prizes, educational | ✅ Safe |
| **Data** | Derived signals, consent-based, erasable | ✅ POPIA Compliant |
| **Finance** | Advisory insights, human interpretation | ✅ Supportive |
| **Credit** | No automated decisions, educational context | ✅ Ethical |
