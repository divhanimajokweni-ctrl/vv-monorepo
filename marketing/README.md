## ⚖️ Legal & Compliance

### Regulatory Readiness
- **POPIA Compliance:** All materials emphasize consent, erasure rights, and ethical data use
- **Gambling Classification:** Games positioned as educational simulations, not gambling
- **Financial Regulation:** Advisory insights only, no automated decision-making

### Key Legal Protections
- **No real stakes** in any game activities
- **Derived data only** (no raw personal information)
- **Educational purpose** clearly established
- **Voluntary participation** throughout
- **Erasure rights** maintained

### Risk Mitigation
- **Cultural sensitivity** built into all messaging
- **Ethical boundaries** clearly defined
- **Exit options** provided at every stage
- **Community control** emphasized
