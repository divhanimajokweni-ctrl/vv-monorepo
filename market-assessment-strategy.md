## SECTION 1: VALIDATED MARKET ASSESSMENT

### 1.1 The Primary Market: South Africa's Stokvel Economy

The quantified opportunity is larger and more urgent than most board decks claim, because the numbers are now publicly confirmed:

South Africa has an estimated 11 million stokvel members, collectively saving USD 3 billion (approximately R50 billion) in 820,000 stokvels annually. This is not a projected figure — it is a counted reality. The opportunity is not to create a new behaviour; it is to provide the infrastructure for a behaviour that already exists at scale.

The acceleration signal is equally concrete. According to First National Bank, stokvel account balances soared by 66% year-on-year, reaching more than R13 billion by December 2024, compared to R8 billion in 2023. This growth occurred without a dedicated platform built on Ubuntu principles — it occurred because FNB removed digital friction from existing stokvel behaviour. Ubuntu Pools offers something FNB cannot: governance, reputation, and collective intelligence built specifically for the stokvel structure rather than retrofitted onto a corporate bank account.

The interest and efficiency case is now quantified. In 2025 alone, stokvels earned R256 million in interest through digitisation, money that would have been forfeited had those funds remained outside the formal system. Stokvels also avoided R211 million in withdrawal fees by reducing reliance on cash and using digital channels. This is the financial proof of concept Ubuntu Pools needs in every pitch: digitisation of informal savings produces direct, measurable value for members.

### 1.2 The Broader Fintech Market Context

The South Africa fintech market size reached USD 981.32 million in 2024 and is projected to reach USD 3,688.72 million by 2033, exhibiting a growth rate of 15.85% during 2025–2033.

Nearly 500 million adults in the Middle East and Africa region are unbanked — that is 52% — while 43% are underbanked. Ubuntu Pools is not a product for a niche; it is a product for the majority of the continent's adult population.

The competitive signal: most Africans' first interaction with the financial services sector may be through their smartphones, presenting major fintech opportunities in payments and lending for regional champions with full-stack attacker models. Ubuntu Pools is designed as a full-stack attacker — not a payments layer on top of a bank, but a complete financial operating system for villages.

### 1.3 Competitive Landscape Assessment

The current digital stokvel market is characterised by toolkit players, not platform players:

**StokFella** — 8-year incumbent, positioned as a digital administration tool. Manages existing stokvels digitally but does not provide credit, governance intelligence, or behavioural reputation. Revenue model: subscription fees. No Lindiwe-equivalent intelligence layer.

**Circles 360 (StepLadder)** — UK-origin platform launched for South Africa, entrepreneur-focused. Can manage up to 1,000 participants and offers partnership revenue through a 'Reason for Saving' feature that groups members by savings goals to enable targeted offers. Closer in model to Ubuntu Pools, but no credit facilities, no Ubuntu Score equivalent, no POPIA-compliant sovereignty layer.

**NASASA** — Self-regulatory body, not a technology platform. Operates as a learning organisation and has introduced savings bonds and funeral plans. Potential regulatory partner, not a competitor.

**FNB/Nedbank digital stokvel accounts** — Bank-native solutions. Nedbank's Stokvel Savings Account offers interest rates of 4.25% to 6% per annum, can be opened with R100, and includes optional funeral insurance at R25 per member per month. These are compelling for the deposit layer but offer zero governance intelligence, zero collective credit, and zero reputation portability. They are the infrastructure layer Ubuntu Pools can build on, not compete with.

**Ubuntu Pools' moat** is the combination: collective governance + reputation + credit + behavioural intelligence + sovereignty controls. No competitor has all five. Most have one.

### 1.4 Regulatory Landscape — Critical Window

FSCA Conduct Standard 3 of 2025 was published on 14 August 2025 and will come into effect 12 months after the date of publication, on 15 August 2026. This creates a defined window: Ubuntu Pools must achieve NASASA alignment and begin FSCA Regulatory Sandbox engagement before August 2026 to avoid operating in ambiguity.

The FSCA Regulatory Sandbox is the correct entry point. The Regulatory Sandbox provides a live testing environment that balances the risk of new market participants and innovative solutions that cannot be addressed within the current regulatory framework, whilst enjoying the benefits of innovative disruption. An application to the FSCA Sandbox in Q3 2026 — backed by 90 days of pilot data from real villages — is the regulatory strategy.

POPIA compliance is non-negotiable and enforcement is active. The maximum POPIA compliance penalty is R10 million, and criminal prosecution in severe cases is possible. The 2025 Amendment Regulations require all security compromises to be reported as soon as the organisation is reasonably sure, not after full investigation. The platform's existing sovereignty layer is a genuine competitive advantage here, but it must be operationally enforced, not just architecturally described.

## SECTION 2: PROFIT ASSESSMENT & REVENUE ARCHITECTURE

### 2.1 Revenue Streams — Current Operational Status

Ubuntu Pools has four viable revenue streams at current platform maturity. Each is assessed against what the codebase can actually support today versus what requires the spine to be proven first.

**Stream 1: Transaction Fee Revenue (Ready after spine proof)**
- Model: 0.5% to 1.5% platform fee on all pool contributions processed through Dodo Payments
- TAM at 1% penetration of R50B stokvel market: R500 million AUM → R5 million annual fee revenue
- Activation condition: ledger invariants enforced, idempotency proven, Dodo webhook reliability confirmed
- Time to first revenue: 60–90 days post spine proof

**Stream 2: Credit Facility Margin (Ready Q4 2026)**
- Model: Ubuntu Pools originates micro-credit to verified village members at pool-informed credit limits. Margin between cost of capital and lending rate: 8–14% annually
- The Ubuntu Score creates an underwriting signal that traditional credit bureaus cannot replicate for this population segment
- Activation condition: credit schema proven against real contribution data, NASASA alignment, FSCA sandbox entry
- TAM: even at 5% lending penetration of 11 million members at average R2,000 credit facility = R1.1 billion loan book potential

**Stream 3: Behavioural Intelligence / Data Products (Ready Q1 2027)**
- Model: anonymised, aggregated population-level financial behaviour signals licensed to insurers, development finance institutions, and microfinance lenders
- Price point: R500K–R2M annual data partnership agreements
- Legal prerequisite: POPIA-compliant derived-signals framework (sovereignty proxy already architecturally in place; needs operational verification)
- The Lindiwe behavioural signal pipeline is the most defensible long-term asset in the platform

**Stream 4: B2B SaaS / White Label (Ready Q2 2027)**
- Model: licence the Ubuntu Pools governance and reputation engine to credit unions, burial societies, and NGOs running community finance programmes
- Price: R5,000–R50,000/month per enterprise client based on member count
- Market: 800,000 stokvels, 14,000+ credit unions, burial societies across Southern Africa

### 3.3 Infrastructure Operational Considerations

**Vercel (current):** Appropriate for development and early pilot. Limitation: serverless cold starts are incompatible with real-time ROSCA payout events that require guaranteed execution. Migration trigger: first village with a live payout cycle.

**PostgreSQL via Neon/Supabase (current):** Adequate for pilot scale. The `balanced_posting` CHECK constraint and `projection_lag` view added in the spine migration are the minimum viable production DB hardening. Migration trigger: 50+ concurrent villages.

**Redis (Upstash, current):** Adequate for session and rate limiting. Not adequate for the real-time event queue needed when the worker process handles payout coordination. Migration trigger: 20+ villages with overlapping payout cycles.

**WhatsApp Business API (current):** Rate limits at 1,000 messages/day on standard tier. Adequate for 50-village pilot. Migration trigger: Business Solution Provider tier engagement needed before 200 villages.

### 4.1 Operational Roles — Minimum Viable Team

The platform can be operated by three people through the 10-village phase:

**Technical Lead (1):** Owns spine health, deployment, incident response. Daily task: review `/api/health/spine` at 08:00 and 20:00. Weekly task: run `./scripts/run-spine.sh` against staging. On-call: PagerDuty or equivalent alert from Sentry on `status=degraded`.

**Community Lead (1):** Owns village onboarding, member support via WhatsApp, friction log. Daily task: review friction log at admin dashboard. Weekly task: 30-minute check-in with each pilot village administrator.

**Compliance & Finance Lead (1):** Owns POPIA consent records, Dodo Payments reconciliation, NASASA relationship. Daily task: review transaction reconciliation report. Monthly task: POPIA consent audit against active member list.

### 4.3 Incident Response Protocol

**P0 — Village money at risk:** Balanced posting constraint violated OR projection lag > 5 minutes. Response: immediate rollback to last known good state, notify all affected village administrators within 15 minutes via WhatsApp.

**P1 — Spine degraded:** `/api/health/spine` returns 503. Response: within 30 minutes, Tech Lead investigates via Sentry, deploys fix or activates maintenance mode.

**P2 — Feature unavailable:** Games, Lindiwe, CPME offline. Response: within 4 hours. These are frozen packages — their failure does not affect core financial operations.

**P3 — Notification failure:** WhatsApp dispatch fails. Response: within 24 hours. The spine's non-fatal notification design means no financial data is lost; the notification queue is replayed from event log.

### Agent 1: Daily Spine Health Monitor

```yaml
# .claude/agents/spine-health-monitor.yml
name: spine-health-monitor
schedule: "0 8,20 * * *"   # 08:00 and 20:00 daily
description: >
  Check all spine health indicators. Alert on any degradation.
  Write results to logs/spine-health-YYYY-MM-DD-HH.json.

tasks:
  - id: check-health-endpoint
    action: http_get
    url: "${NEXT_PUBLIC_URL}/api/health/spine"
    expect_status: [200, 503]
    on_failure: alert

  - id: check-projection-lag
    action: sql_query
    query: >
      SELECT village_id, lag_seconds, unprocessed_events
      FROM projection_lag
      WHERE lag_seconds > 30 OR unprocessed_events > 0
    on_results: alert_with_context

  - id: check-audit-completeness
    action: sql_query
    query: >
      SELECT COUNT(*) as events_without_trace
      FROM domain_events de
      LEFT JOIN audit_log al ON al.event_id = de.id
      WHERE al.id IS NULL
        AND de.created_at > NOW() - INTERVAL '24 hours'
    threshold: 0
    on_exceed: alert

  - id: write-report
    action: write_json
    path: "logs/spine-health-${TIMESTAMP}.json"
    include: [check-health-endpoint, check-projection-lag, check-audit-completeness]

alerts:
  channels:
    - type: sentry
      level: error
    - type: whatsapp
      recipient: "${TECH_LEAD_PHONE}"
      message: "Spine degraded: {summary}. Check /api/health/spine"
```

### Agent 2: Daily Transaction Reconciliation

```yaml
# .claude/agents/daily-reconciliation.yml
name: daily-reconciliation
schedule: "0 6 * * *"   # 06:00 daily, before business hours
description: >
  Reconcile Dodo Payments webhooks against ledger entries.
  Flag any contribution that has a Dodo payment record
  but no corresponding balanced ledger entry.

tasks:
  - id: fetch-dodo-transactions
    action: http_get
    url: "https://api.dodopayments.com/v1/transactions"
    headers:
      Authorization: "Bearer ${DODO_PAYMENTS_CLIENT_SECRET}"
    params:
      from: "${YESTERDAY_ISO}"
      to: "${TODAY_ISO}"

  - id: cross-reference-ledger
    action: sql_query
    query: >
      SELECT
        pc.idempotency_key,
        pc.status,
        pc.amount_minor_units,
        le.id as ledger_entry_id,
        le.total_debits,
        le.total_credits
      FROM pool_contributions pc
      LEFT JOIN ledger_entries le ON le.event_id = pc.id
      WHERE pc.created_at >= NOW() - INTERVAL '24 hours'
        AND (le.id IS NULL OR pc.status != 'POSTED')

  - id: flag-discrepancies
    action: compare
    source: fetch-dodo-transactions
    target: cross-reference-ledger
    match_on: idempotency_key
    on_mismatch: write_to_friction_log

  - id: write-reconciliation-report
    action: write_json
    path: "logs/reconciliation-${TIMESTAMP}.json"

  - id: alert-if-discrepancies
    action: conditional_alert
    condition: "discrepancy_count > 0"
    message: "Reconciliation found {discrepancy_count} unmatched transactions. Review logs/reconciliation-${TIMESTAMP}.json"
    channels: [sentry, whatsapp_finance_lead]
```

### Agent 4: Monthly POPIA Consent Audit

```yaml
# .claude/agents/monthly-popia-audit.yml
name: monthly-popia-audit
schedule: "0 9 1 * *"   # 1st of each month at 09:00
description: >
  Audit POPIA consent records against active member list.
  Flag members who lack valid consent records.
  Generate report for Compliance Lead review.

tasks:
  - id: active-members-without-consent
    action: sql_query
    query: >
      SELECT m.id, m.created_at, m.village_id
      FROM members m
      WHERE m.is_active = true
        AND NOT EXISTS (
          SELECT 1 FROM consent_records cr
          WHERE cr.member_id = m.id
            AND cr.consent_type = 'POPIA_DATA_PROCESSING'
            AND cr.is_valid = true
            AND cr.expires_at > NOW()
        )

  - id: erasure-requests-pending
    action: sql_query
    query: >
      SELECT COUNT(*) as pending_erasures
      FROM sovereignty_requests
      WHERE status = 'PENDING'
        AND created_at < NOW() - INTERVAL '30 days'

  - id: data-retention-check
    action: sql_query
    query: >
      SELECT COUNT(*) as overdue_records
      FROM domain_events
      WHERE created_at < NOW() - INTERVAL '5 years'
        AND NOT EXISTS (
          SELECT 1 FROM retention_exemptions re
          WHERE re.event_id = domain_events.id
        )

  - id: generate-compliance-report
    action: write_json
    path: "logs/popia-audit-${MONTH}-${YEAR}.json"
    include: [active-members-without-consent, erasure-requests-pending, data-retention-check]
```

### Agent 5: Projection Lag Auto-Repair

```yaml
# .claude/agents/projection-lag-repair.yml
name: projection-lag-repair
trigger: webhook   # triggered by /api/health/spine returning lag status
description: >
  When projection lag exceeds threshold, identify the
  unprocessed events and replay them through the projection pipeline.
  Do NOT modify financial data — only refresh derived state.

tasks:
  - id: identify-stale-villages
    action: sql_query
    query: >
      SELECT village_id, last_event_id, lag_seconds, unprocessed_events
      FROM projection_lag
      WHERE lag_seconds > 30 OR unprocessed_events > 0
      ORDER BY lag_seconds DESC

  - id: fetch-unprocessed-events
    action: sql_query
    query: >
      SELECT de.*
      FROM domain_events de
      JOIN projections p ON p.village_id = de.village_id
      WHERE de.created_at > p.refreshed_at
        AND de.village_id = ANY(${stale_village_ids})
      ORDER BY de.created_at ASC

  - id: replay-projections
    action: api_call
    for_each: fetch-unprocessed-events
    endpoint: "internal://projection/replay"
    payload:
      eventId: "${event.id}"
      villageId: "${event.village_id}"
    on_error: log_and_continue   # do not abort — replay as many as possible

  - id: verify-repair
    action: sql_query
    query: >
      SELECT COUNT(*) as remaining_lag
      FROM projection_lag
      WHERE lag_seconds > 30 OR unprocessed_events > 0

  - id: post-repair-report
    action: conditional_alert
    condition: "remaining_lag > 0"
    message: "Projection lag repair incomplete. {remaining_lag} villages still lagging."
    channels: [sentry, whatsapp_tech_lead]
```
