06 · Brand Application & Spatial Rules

Clearspace / Padding

Logo requires clearspace = 1x the height of the interlocking circle mark. Minimum size: 48px width for digital.

Motion & Interaction

Gentle easing (0.2s) on hover states. No aggressive animations — reflect stability & Ubuntu rhythm.

Typographic Hierarchy

H1: 2rem/800 · H2: 1.5rem/700 · H3: 1.25rem/600. Body text never < 0.875rem. All caps sparingly — only for labels.

Accessibility

Focus rings: 2px solid var(--vvu-ochre). Avoid low contrast on CTA.
